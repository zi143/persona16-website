Frequently Asked Questions

How does Persona16 work?

Persona16 offers a 32-question personality exploration experience based on four personality preference dimensions. After completing the test, you can explore your personality type and detailed reports.

Does Persona16 require an internet connection?

The core personality test and reports work offline. An internet connection may be required for optional in-app purchases and certain Apple services.

Can I change the app language?

Persona16 supports English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, and Spanish. You can select your preferred language using the app's language controls.

What if an in-app purchase fails?

Check your internet connection and ensure that your Apple Account can make purchases. If a transaction is interrupted, review your App Store purchase history before trying again.

For billing or refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem website .

Can I retake the personality test?

Yes. Persona16 provides a retake option so you can explore your personality preferences again.

Are personality results a diagnosis?

No. Persona16 is designed for educational purposes and personal reflection. It is not a medical or psychological diagnostic service.
