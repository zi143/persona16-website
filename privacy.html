Persona16 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Persona16 is a personality exploration application offering a 32-question personality test, insights into 16 personality types, personal growth guidance, and career development perspectives for the AI era.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the core personality exploration experience on your device. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Persona16.

1. Personality Tests and Reports

Personality test responses, scoring, personality type determination, and report presentation are processed locally on your device.

Persona16 does not transmit your test answers or personality results to developer-operated servers. The personality reports and supported language resources are included within the application.

2. Data Collection

In the current version, Persona16 does not collect personal information, personality test responses, or personality results through developer-operated servers or third-party analytics services.

Persona16 does not require account registration and does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.

3. AI-Era Development Content

The AI-era personal and career development suggestions are prewritten educational content. Persona16 does not send your test responses to OpenAI or another external AI service to generate these reports in real time.

4. In-App Purchases

Persona16 may offer optional in-app purchases to support the developer. Purchases are processed using Apple's StoreKit and App Store services.

Apple may process payment and transaction information according to its own privacy practices. Persona16 does not directly collect or store your payment card details.

For information about Apple's privacy practices, please visit Apple's Privacy Policy .

5. Sharing Your Results

Persona16 may allow you to share personality results using your device's system sharing features. Sharing is initiated by you.

When you choose an external application or service to share content, that service may process the information according to its own privacy policy.

6. Data Storage and Control

The core personality test and report features operate locally. Persona16 does not maintain a developer-operated cloud database of your personality results.

You can stop using the application or delete it from your device. Deleting the application generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to the device's backup and operating system behavior.

7. Children's Privacy

Persona16 is intended for general educational and personal exploration purposes. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Educational Disclaimer

Persona16 is intended for personal exploration and educational purposes only. Personality frameworks are tools for reflection and do not determine an individual's identity, abilities, career outcomes, or future.

Persona16 is not a clinical, psychological, or medical diagnostic tool.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the application, applicable requirements, or privacy practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
